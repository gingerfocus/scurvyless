atmosphere, price and importantly accessablity by transit.**

### Transit
Transit means more than just system in place around the restraunt, it is also
an effort by them to be easy to arrive at. This can be a factor of many things
but being inviting to enter is key.

### Price
Price is the least important but still a concidered factor. Producing the best
meal likely costs more, wealth can buy status after all but there is a enphasis
on excess placed in the rating. Items that are deemed to be charging too much
to the provided product will be marked slightly.
