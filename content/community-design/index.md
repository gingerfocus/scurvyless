This project was done as part of the ENGR110 Community Based Design Project
with the goal to create a data montiering and analysis tool for insight into
raw sales data. You can check out more specifics about [the project here](./project.html).

## Our Partner
This project is done in collaberation with SCU Access Office who provided use
access to the data as well as guidance on what metrics they find useful. Their
goal is to provide convient and invisable support to the student body by
managing payment servies and food in a way that does. Our critcal customers are
the students as we aim to provide better more target servies.
